This is what a Driftline day looks like

A real three-day opener from our Reykjavik and South Coast itinerary. Every stop carries a time, a hop to the next stop, and a daily cost estimate per person.

Day 1

Land, soak, sleep early

Est. $186 / person
  • 06:30 AM Arrive KEF from Boston or Newark

    Overnight flights land early. Pick up the rental car before passport control lines build.

    → 20 min drive to the lagoon
  • 09:00 AM Blue Lagoon, first entry slot

    Book the earliest slot. It doubles as a shower after the red-eye and kills jet lag better than a nap.

    → 50 min drive to Reykjavik
  • 01:00 PM Late lunch on Laugavegur

    Lamb soup and a flat white. Groceries at Kronan afterward for the road-trip cooler.

    → 10 min walk
  • 04:30 PM Hallgrimskirkja tower and Sun Voyager

    Ninety unhurried minutes. Sunset over the harbor is the whole point.

Driving: 82 miles Walking: 2.4 miles Pace: easy
Day 2

Golden Circle, slowly

Est. $154 / person
  • 08:00 AM Leave Reykjavik after breakfast

    Fill the tank in town. Fuel is meaningfully cheaper than at the rural pumps.

    → 45 min drive
  • 09:00 AM Thingvellir National Park

    Walk the Almannagja rift before the tour coaches arrive at 10:30 AM. Parking is $6, card only.

    → 55 min drive
  • 11:30 AM Geysir geothermal field

    Strokkur erupts every six to ten minutes. Twenty-five minutes is plenty.

    → 12 min drive
  • 01:00 PM Gullfoss and the canyon walk

    Lower platform first, upper viewpoint second. Waterproof shell, not an umbrella.

    → 75 min drive to Hella
  • 06:30 PM Check in near Hella, dinner in

    Guesthouses along Route 1 are cheaper than Selfoss and put you ahead for the south coast.

Driving: 168 miles Walking: 3.1 miles Pace: moderate
Day 3

South coast waterfalls and black sand

Est. $171 / person
  • 08:15 AM Seljalandsfoss

    The path behind the falls soaks you. Pack the rain pants you swore you would not need.

    → 25 min drive
  • 10:00 AM Skogafoss and the first 400 steps

    You do not have to hike the whole Fimmvorduhals trail. The first viewpoint earns its keep.

    → 35 min drive
  • 12:15 PM Lunch in Vik

    Last reliable groceries before the sand plains. Fill the tank here too.

    → 10 min drive
  • 02:00 PM Reynisfjara black sand beach

    Stay well back from the water. Sneaker waves here are genuinely dangerous, not a marketing line.

    → 20 min drive
  • 08:30 PM Aurora check from the guesthouse

    September through March only. Check the Icelandic Met Office forecast before you commit to staying up.

Driving: 134 miles Walking: 4.6 miles Pace: moderate

How we make money, in plain English

We would rather tell you this on the homepage than bury it in a footer.

  1. Subscriptions. The free plan covers two trips. Pro costs $9 per month or $84 per year and removes the trip limit, adds PDF export and collaboration.
  2. Affiliate commissions. Some hotel, flight, car rental and tour links pay us a commission when you book. The price you pay does not change. Those links are labeled where they appear.
  3. Custom itinerary service. A one-time fee for a human-reviewed plan, starting at $149 for trips up to seven days.
  4. Agent and white-label plans. Travel advisors and small tour operators pay a monthly fee to build client-facing itineraries under their own brand.
  5. Advertising. Some pages carry clearly labeled ad slots. Ads never sit above the main content of a page and are never dressed up as editorial recommendations.
