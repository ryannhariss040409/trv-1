Plans and pricing

Yes. The free plan covers two saved trips with up to 14 days each and it does not expire or convert into a paid trial. Every destination guide, sample itinerary, packing checklist and the budget estimator is free and does not require an account at all.

Pro exists for people who plan more than two trips, want a PDF for offline use, or want to edit a plan together with the people they are traveling with.

Pro is $9 per month or $84 per year, which works out to $7 per month. Cancel from your account settings and Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. There is no cancellation fee and no retention call.

Refunds within 14 days of a charge are covered by our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

Nothing is deleted. If you have five trips and drop to free, all five stay readable, printable and exportable. You can actively edit two until you either archive some or upgrade again. We do not lock work you already did behind a paywall.

Yes. Pro is $4 per month for students with a valid .edu address and for registered US nonprofits. Email hello@driftline.travel from that address and we will apply it manually. There is no form.

Trust, money and independence

No. We are a software company, not a travel agency, tour operator or seller of travel, and we hold no such registrations. When we link to a booking site you transact directly with that provider under their terms. See our Disclaimer.

Yes, on some hotel, flight, car rental and tour links. If you book through one, the partner pays us a commission and the price you pay is unchanged. Those links carry a visible label at the point of use, and a commission never affects which places we recommend or the order they appear in. Full details in our Affiliate Disclosure.

No. There is no price at which a property can buy a mention in a where-to-stay section. We have turned this down repeatedly and we will keep turning it down, because a paid recommendation makes every unpaid one worthless.

They come from a survey we send after a planned trip ends, they are published with permission, and we do not edit the wording. We do not buy reviews, incentivize them, or write them internally. Where a reviewer asked to be identified only by first name and city, that is what appears.

Advertising helps fund the free tier. Ad slots are clearly labeled, sit below the main content of a page, never touch navigation or buttons, and never appear on legal pages or the error page. Pro subscribers see no ads on planner pages. Advertising cookies stay switched off until you accept them in the consent banner.

Privacy, data and accessibility

If you never create an account: essentially nothing beyond standard server logs and, if you accept them, analytics and advertising cookies. If you create an account: your email address, your trips, and billing records held by our payment processor. The complete list, including third parties and retention periods, is in our Privacy Policy.

No. We do not sell personal information for money. Because California law defines sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising broadly, we provide a Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information control anyway, and it is linked in the footer of every page.

Email privacy@driftline.travel from the address on the account. We confirm within 10 business days and complete deletion within 45 days, excluding records we are legally required to keep such as transaction receipts for tax purposes.

No. Driftline is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, in line with COPPA. If we learn that we have, we delete it. Parents who believe a child has created an account should email privacy@driftline.travel.

We build to WCAG 2.1 Level AA: semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus states, keyboard operation throughout, alt text on every image, a 4.5:1 minimum text contrast ratio, and full support for reduced-motion preferences. If something does not work for you, tell us and we treat it as a bug rather than a feature request. See the Accessibility Statement.
