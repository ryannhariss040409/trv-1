Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 08/01/2026. All amounts in US dollars. This policy forms part of our Terms of Service.

The short version: cancel any subscription at any time from account settings. Ask for a refund within 14 days of a charge and you get it. For the custom itinerary service, you get a full refund before we start work and a partial refund up to first delivery.

1. Free plan

There is nothing to cancel and nothing to refund. The free plan costs nothing, does not expire, and does not require a payment method. You can stop using it at any time and delete your account from settings.

2. Driftline Pro, monthly and annual

2.1 Cancelling

Cancel at any time in account settings under Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, so a monthly subscriber who cancels on day 3 keeps Pro for the remaining 27 days. There is no cancellation fee and no retention call.

2.2 Refund window

We do not require a reason. If you tell us one it helps us improve the product, but it is not a condition of the refund.

2.3 What happens to your trips

Nothing is deleted when you cancel or downgrade. If you have more trips than the free plan allows, all of them stay readable, printable and exportable. You can actively edit two at a time until you archive some or upgrade again.

3. Custom itinerary service

This is a one-time service with a human doing research on your behalf, so the terms differ from a subscription.

"Does not match your brief" means the plan ignores something you told us in the questionnaire, such as a stated budget ceiling, an accessibility requirement, or a place you asked us to avoid. It does not mean you changed your mind about the destination after reading the plan.

The optional $79 planning call is refundable in full if cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled time.

4. Agent plans, Studio and White-label

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. Cancel in account settings or by emailing agents@driftline.travel.
  • Refunds follow the same 14-day rule as Pro. Annual agent plans are refundable pro-rata for unused whole months after that window.
  • Client continuity. Your account stays readable and exportable for 60 days after the paid period ends, and client share links keep working for any trip whose end date has not yet passed. Nobody is stranded mid-trip because of a billing decision.
  • Operator plans are governed by the individually signed agreement, which takes precedence over this page where the two differ.

5. One-off purchases

The $6 single-trip PDF export is refundable within 7 days if the export failed or produced an unusable file. If the file downloaded correctly and you simply did not need it, we cannot refund it, because the product was delivered.

6. What we cannot refund

Driftline does not sell travel, so we have no ability to refund anything you paid to a third party. That includes flights, lodging, car rentals, tours, park permits and travel insurance, whether or not you reached that provider through a link on our site.

For those, contact the provider directly. Their cancellation terms apply. If you booked through an affiliate link on our site, that link earned us a small commission but gave us no access to your booking and no ability to change it. See our Disclaimer.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@driftline.travel from the address on the account, with "Refund request" in the subject line.
  2. Include the approximate charge date and amount. You do not need an invoice number, though it helps.
  3. We acknowledge within 2 business days and approve or explain within 5 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank typically posts them within 5 to 10 business days after we send them.

If you prefer to write, send your request to Billing, Driftline Travel Co., 1201 Ocean Avenue, Suite 410, Santa Monica, CA 90401. Postal requests take longer for obvious reasons.

8. Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback with your bank before contacting us, the account is suspended automatically while the dispute is open, because our payment processor requires it. We would much rather resolve it directly, and in practice we approve nearly every refund request we receive. Please email us first.

9. Price changes and renewals

We email you at least 30 days before an annual renewal charge and at least 30 days before any price increase applies to your account. If you do not want the renewal, cancel before that date and nothing is charged. If a renewal charge surprises you despite that notice, ask for a refund within 14 days and we will issue it.

10. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer protection law, including California law. Where a statutory right gives you more than this policy does, the statutory right applies.
