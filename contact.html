Where we are

Our office sits four blocks from the Santa Monica pier, on the fourth floor of a building on Ocean Avenue. We are a small team and we do not have a walk-in reception, so please email before visiting rather than arriving unannounced.

Mail sent to the address above reaches us. Legal notices should be sent by certified mail and also emailed to hello@driftline.travel so we can acknowledge receipt promptly.

For data access, deletion or opt-out requests under California law, use privacy@driftline.travel or the Do Not Sell or Share section of our Privacy Policy. That route is monitored specifically for those requests and is the fastest way to get one processed.
