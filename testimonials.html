How we collect and publish reviews

Fourteen days after the end date of a plan you built, we send one survey email. It asks for a star rating, what worked, and what did not. There is no incentive attached: no discount, no entry into anything, no free month.

We ask permission before publishing and we publish the wording as written, correcting only obvious typos. Reviewers choose whether to appear by full name or first name and city. Nobody on our team writes reviews, and we have never purchased one.

We publish critical reviews alongside positive ones because a page of nothing but five stars tells you nothing. Roughly 11 percent of our survey responses are three stars or below, and you can filter for them above.

When a review points at a factual error, such as the Maui lodging band above, we fix the underlying data and note the update date on the guide. That Maui band was revised on 07/14/2025.
