How these fifteen were chosen

They are the destinations our readers actually search for, cross-checked against three things: a reasonable flight from a major US hub, a trip that works inside one to two weeks of PTO, and enough public information that we can publish cost bands we are willing to stand behind.

Cost bands assume two travelers sharing a room, mid-range lodging, and economy airfare booked six to ten weeks out. They are researched estimates, refreshed at least twice per year, not live quotes. Airfare in particular moves fast.

Missing somewhere obvious? We add roughly one destination guide per month, driven by reader requests. Send us a suggestion.

What is in every full guide

  • Overview and who the destination suits
  • Best time to visit, month by month
  • Budget breakdown table in USD
  • A three-day sample plan with times and costs
  • Getting around, including whether to rent a car
  • Where to stay, by neighborhood or town
  • Safety notes and US government advisory context
