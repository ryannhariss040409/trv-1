One-time service

Custom itinerary service

If you would rather hand the work to a person, our itinerary team builds the plan for you. You answer a questionnaire about dates, pace, budget and what you actually enjoy. We research, route and cost the trip, then send it back as a Driftline plan and a PDF.

This is a research and planning service. We do not book anything on your behalf and we do not take commission on what you book afterward, which is deliberate: it removes any incentive to steer you toward a particular hotel.

Optional add-ons and how we make money

Nothing here is required to use Driftline, and none of it changes what the planner does.

Travel insurance referrals

We refer travelers to a licensed insurance partner and receive a referral fee if you buy a policy. We are not an insurance broker and we do not advise on coverage. Buying through the referral is entirely optional.

How referrals work

Offline PDF export

Included with Pro rather than sold as a separate purchase. Free plans can still print from the browser, which produces a clean paper copy with navigation and ads removed.

One-off PDF export for a single trip on a free plan costs $6.

Affiliate commissions

Some hotel, flight, car rental and tour links pay us a commission when you book through them. The price you pay is the same either way, and those links carry a visible label at the point of use.

We never accept payment for placement inside a destination guide.

Plain-English summary

Subscriptions are the largest and fastest-growing part of our revenue, and they are the only part that is not tied to you booking something. That is why they fund the guides. Affiliate commissions and advertising are secondary, and the custom itinerary service is priced as a flat fee precisely so that nobody on our team benefits from recommending an expensive hotel over a good one.

If you ever see something on this site that reads like a recommendation but feels like an ad, tell us at hello@driftline.travel. We treat that as a bug.
