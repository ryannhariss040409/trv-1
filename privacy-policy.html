Privacy Policy

Last updated 08/01/2026. Effective 08/01/2026.

Who we are. Driftline Travel Co. ("Driftline", "we", "us") operates the website at www.driftline.travel and the Driftline trip planner. Our mailing address is 1201 Ocean Avenue, Suite 410, Santa Monica, CA 90401, United States. You can reach us at privacy@driftline.travel or +1 (310) 555-0147.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the Driftline planner. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to, including booking sites, which have their own policies.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information. Email address and a password hash if you create an account. We never store passwords in readable form.
  • Trip content. Trip names, days, stops, times, notes and cost figures you enter. If you do not create an account, this stays in your browser and never reaches our servers.
  • Contact form and email. Name, email address, optional phone number, the topic you select, and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter signup. Your email address only.
  • Custom itinerary service. The answers you provide in the intake questionnaire, which may include travel dates, party composition, budget range and accessibility needs you choose to share.
  • Payment information. Handled by our third-party payment processor. We receive a transaction record, the last four digits of the card and its brand. We never receive or store the full card number.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. IP address, user agent string, referring URL, pages requested and timestamps. These are collected as a necessary part of serving the site and for security.
  • Analytics data, only if you consent: pages viewed, approximate region derived from IP, device type, and which features were used. We configure IP truncation where the provider supports it.
  • Advertising data, only if you consent: identifiers used by advertising vendors to select and measure ads.
  • Local storage. Your consent choice, and, if you allow functional storage, your saved trips and packing checklist state. Local storage lives on your device and is not transmitted to us.

1.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, or any special category data. We do not ask for your passport number and you should not send it to us.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the planner, save your trips, and deliver the guides and tools you request.
  • To process payments and provide receipts for paid plans and the custom itinerary service.
  • To answer support, sales, press and privacy inquiries.
  • To send the newsletter, if you asked for it, and to send transactional messages about your account.
  • To measure aggregate usage so we know which guides to update, only with analytics consent.
  • To display and measure advertising, only with advertising consent.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting record keeping.

We do not use your trip content to train machine learning models, and we do not read your trips except when you explicitly ask support to look at a specific plan.

3. Legal bases, for visitors in the EEA and UK

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on: contract, to provide the service you signed up for; consent, for analytics cookies, advertising cookies and the newsletter; legitimate interests, for security, fraud prevention and basic server logging; and legal obligation, for tax and accounting records. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Full detail, including named categories and durations, is in our Cookie Policy.

  • Strictly necessary. Session security, form protection, and a record of the consent choice you made. These are always active because the site cannot function without them.
  • Functional. Saves trips and checklist state in your browser. Off until you allow it.
  • Analytics. Aggregated usage measurement. Off until you allow it.
  • Advertising. Ad selection and measurement, including cookies set by Google. Off until you allow it.

No analytics or advertising cookies are set before you make a choice in the consent banner. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

6. When we share information

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • With service providers listed above, under contract, only for the purpose we engaged them for.
  • With a travel advisor, if you are a client using an agent's white-label account. In that case the advisor is the controller of your trip data and their own privacy policy applies alongside ours.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of Driftline, our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer, if Driftline is involved in a merger, acquisition or asset sale. We will post notice on this page before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not disclose personal information to data brokers.

8. Security

We serve the site over HTTPS, store passwords using an industry standard one-way hashing algorithm, restrict internal access to personal information on a need-to-know basis, and require multi-factor authentication for staff accounts that can reach production systems. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Driftline is intended for adults planning travel. The service is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. You must be at least 13 to create an account, and at least 18 to purchase a paid plan.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided us with personal information should contact privacy@driftline.travel and we will act within 10 business days.

10. California privacy rights, CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you specific rights.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months

  • Identifiers: name, email address, IP address, account identifier.
  • Commercial information: subscription and purchase records.
  • Internet activity: pages viewed, features used, referring page.
  • Geolocation: approximate region derived from IP address only. We do not collect precise location.
  • Inferences: travel interests inferred from the guides you view, only where advertising consent is given.

We collect these for the business purposes described in section 2. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined by the CPRA.

Your rights

  • Right to know what personal information we collected, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions such as tax records.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect any, so there is nothing to limit.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny service, charge a different price or provide a lesser quality of service because you exercised a right.

To make a request, email privacy@driftline.travel with the subject line "California privacy request", or write to the mailing address above. We verify requests by asking you to confirm from the email address on the account, or by asking two data points that match our records. We confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days where necessary, with notice to you. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written permission.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Driftline does not sell personal information for money. California law defines "sharing" broadly to include disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because our advertising cookies could fall within that definition when you consent to them, we provide this control for everyone regardless of residency.

To opt out of any sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising:

  1. Open Cookie Settings and switch the Advertising category off, then save. This takes effect immediately in this browser.
  2. Or enable Global Privacy Control in your browser or extension. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request for that browser.
  3. Or email privacy@driftline.travel with the subject line "Do not sell or share" and we will apply it to your account.

Opting out does not remove advertising from the site. It stops your personal information being used to select which advertising you see.

Shine the Light

California Civil Code section 1798.83 permits California residents to request information about disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.

11. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of states including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and Montana have comparable rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of their personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact route as section 10 and tell us your state of residence. Where a state law provides an appeal process for a refused request, you may appeal by replying to our decision email with the word "appeal", and we will respond within 45 days.

12. Note for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Driftline is a US company and our audience is primarily in the United States, but we welcome visitors from anywhere. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Personal data you provide is processed in the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with supplementary measures, or on an adequacy decision where one applies. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

13. Your choices

  • Cookies. Change your consent at any time with the Cookie Settings link in the footer.
  • Newsletter. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing does not affect transactional messages about your account.
  • Account deletion. Email privacy@driftline.travel from the address on the account.
  • Browser controls. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working.
  • Do Not Track. There is no consistent industry standard for handling browser DNT signals, so we do not respond to them. We do honor Global Privacy Control.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. When we make a material change we update the "last updated" date at the top, and for significant changes affecting how we use your personal information we will email account holders at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.
