1. General information only

Everything published on www.driftline.travel is provided for general informational and planning purposes. It is written carefully and edited by people who have taken the trips in question, but it is not professional advice and should not be treated as a substitute for it.

You are responsible for your own travel decisions. Use our guides as a starting point, verify anything that matters against an official source, and apply your own judgment about your fitness, experience and risk tolerance.

2. We are not a travel agency

Driftline Travel Co. is a software company. We do not sell, book, reserve, ticket or hold funds for flights, lodging, car rentals, tours, cruises or any other travel product. We are not a seller of travel, a tour operator, a travel agent or an air carrier, and we hold no registrations in those categories because we do not perform those functions.

When you follow a link from our site to a booking provider, your contract is with that provider. Their cancellation, change, refund and liability terms apply, not ours. We have no ability to modify, cancel or refund a booking you make elsewhere.

3. Prices and cost estimates

All prices on this site are shown in US dollars. Cost bands in destination guides and itineraries are researched planning estimates, refreshed at least twice a year, with the update date shown at the top of the page. They are not quotes and they are not guaranteed.

Airfare in particular moves quickly and can differ substantially from any published band. Exchange rates change. Local prices change. Always confirm current pricing with the provider before making a decision that depends on it.

Inside the trip planner, totals are calculated from the figures you enter. We do not fetch live prices and the arithmetic is only as accurate as your inputs.

4. Safety, health and entry requirements

Our guides include safety notes because leaving them out would be worse. They are not comprehensive risk assessments and conditions change constantly.

Before you travel, check these official sources for your destination:

  • The US Department of State country information page and travel advisory level.
  • The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention travel health notices and vaccination guidance.
  • The official immigration authority of the country you are entering, for visa and pre-authorization requirements.
  • The National Park Service or equivalent land manager, for permits, closures and current conditions.
  • Local weather, road and avalanche authorities, for anything weather-dependent.

Outdoor activities described on this site, including hiking, driving on remote roads, swimming, snorkeling and glacier travel, carry inherent risks including serious injury and death. You participate at your own risk and are responsible for assessing your own capability. Where a guide notes a specific hazard, such as sneaker waves at Reynisfjara or exposure on Angels Landing, treat that as a warning rather than a description.

5. Medical, legal, financial and insurance matters

Nothing on this site is medical advice, legal advice, financial advice, tax advice or insurance advice. We are not licensed to provide any of these. Consult a qualified professional for questions about vaccinations, medication legality, visas, taxes on foreign income, or which insurance policy suits your circumstances.

Where we mention travel insurance, we describe categories of coverage in general terms and refer you to a licensed partner. We do not assess your needs, recommend a specific policy, or receive information about what you purchase beyond a referral confirmation. Read any policy document in full before buying.
