COPY NOTES FOR THE REVIEWER
---------------------------
- No superlatives, rankings or unverifiable claims are used anywhere in this set.
- No specific prices, fares or discounts appear. Plan limits such as "two trips"
  are product facts; confirm they still match the live product before launch.
- No airline, hotel, competitor or trademarked brand names are referenced.
- No urgency, scarcity, countdown language or invented statistics.
- "US" and "PTO" are genuine acronyms and are the only capitalized abbreviations.
- No phone numbers, no references to the ad itself, no "click here" language.
- Every line is written to stand alone, because Google mixes assets automatically.
- Angles covered across the set: planning speed, day by day structure, budgeting,
  group and family trips, first time international travelers, road trips, and
  travel from US departure airports.
- Tone matches the site: calm, plain, specific. No exclamation marks.
