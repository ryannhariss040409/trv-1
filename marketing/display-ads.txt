COPY NOTES FOR THE REVIEWER
---------------------------
- No superlatives, rankings, "best", "cheapest" or "guaranteed" language is used.
- No specific prices, fares or discounts. Product facts such as the free tier
  covering two trips must be re-confirmed against the live product before launch.
- No airline, hotel, competitor or trademarked brand names.
- No urgency, scarcity, countdowns or invented statistics.
- "US" and "PTO" are genuine acronyms and are the only capitalized abbreviations.
- No phone numbers, no self-reference to the ad, no "click here" language.
- Every line stands alone, because Google recombines headlines and descriptions.
- Angles covered across the set: planning speed, day by day organization,
  budgeting in USD, group and family trips, first time international travelers,
  road trips, and departures from US airports.
- Image assets should follow the site design direction: soft focus coastal and
  landscape photography, calm aqua palette, generous space, no text baked into
  the image beyond what Google allows.
- Tone matches the site: calm, plain, specific. No exclamation marks.
