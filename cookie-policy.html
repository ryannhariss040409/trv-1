Cookie Policy

Last updated 08/01/2026. This policy sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It can hold a preference, a session identifier or an advertising identifier. Similar technologies include local storage, which keeps data on your device without sending it back to the server on every request, and pixels, which are tiny images used to record that a page loaded.

Driftline uses a small number of cookies and makes heavy use of local storage, because your trip data belongs on your device rather than on ours.

2. Our consent rule, in one sentence

No analytics, advertising or functional storage is used until you choose to allow it. On your first visit the consent banner appears at the bottom of the screen, where it does not block the page. Until you press Accept all or save preferences with a category switched on, only strictly necessary storage is used.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page.

4. How to opt out

  1. Use our control. Open Cookie Settings in the footer, switch categories off, and save. This applies immediately in the current browser.
  2. Use Google's control. Visit Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com to opt out of personalized advertising across Google services.
  3. Use industry controls. The Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info/choices and the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org offer opt-outs covering many vendors at once.
  4. Use Global Privacy Control. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  5. Use your browser. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and clear local storage. Blocking strictly necessary storage will break sign-in and forms.

Opting out of advertising cookies does not remove advertising from the site. It stops your information being used to choose which advertising you see.

5. What happens if you reject everything

Almost everything keeps working. You can read every guide, use the budget estimator, build a trip and print it. What you lose is the ability for a trip or a checklist to survive a page refresh without pressing save, and any personalization of advertising.

6. Cookies set by others

Pages that embed third-party content, such as a mapping widget or a video, may allow that provider to set its own cookies once you interact with the embed. We load such embeds only after advertising or functional consent, whichever applies. Links out to booking providers do not set cookies on our site, but the destination site will set its own once you arrive.

7. Changes to this policy

We review this page whenever we add or remove a vendor, and at minimum every twelve months. The last updated date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects what is stored on your device, the consent banner reappears so you can make a fresh choice.
