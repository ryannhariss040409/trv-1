Who this is for

  • Independent travel advisors running 10 to 200 trips a year
  • Small tour operators with a repeatable set of departures
  • Destination specialists who send custom day plans
  • Corporate retreat and incentive travel planners
  • Agencies that want a client portal without building one

Not a booking engine and not a GDS. Driftline handles the itinerary, not the reservation.

The part of your job that should not take four hours

Advisors we talked to before building this spend between three and six hours per trip on document production. Not research, not selling, not client calls. Formatting.

📋

Templates that carry structure

Save any finished itinerary as a template with the timing, stops and cost structure intact. Next client, load it, change four things, send. The Sedona week takes twelve minutes instead of four hours.

🏷

Your brand, not ours

Studio puts your logo on exported PDFs. White-label goes further: your domain, your colors, and no Driftline branding anywhere the client can see.

💳

Markup notes that stay private

Record your commission and markup per line item in a private field. Clients see the itinerary. You see the margin. The two never appear on the same page.

How advisors use it

Intake. Send the client a short questionnaire link. Answers land in the trip record, so you stop rereading email threads to find out whether they wanted the early flight.

Build. Start from a template or from one of our 120-plus destination guides. Drop in your own suppliers and preferred properties. Costs total as you go, per traveler and per party.

Review. Share a link. The client comments directly on the day they have a question about rather than replying to the whole document. Every change is versioned for 90 days.

Deliver. Export a branded PDF for offline use and keep the live link active for the trip itself. Clients can open it on a phone at an airport gate with no app to install.
