The Utah Five in nine days

Zion, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Arches and Canyonlands on a loop from Las Vegas, with one genuine rest day and no driving day over four hours.

9 daysSeven PTO days across two weekends
$2,175Estimated mid-range cost per person, USD
1,148 miTotal driving from LAS and back
04/10 to 05/31Best window for this exact route

Before you start

  • Buy the America the Beautiful pass, $80. It covers entry for everyone in one vehicle at all five parks and pays for itself at the third gate.
  • Check permits three months out and again two weeks out. Zion's Angels Landing lottery and Arches timed entry both change year to year. Use the official National Park Service pages, not a blog.
  • Book Moab and Springdale early. April and May sell out. Torrey and Tropic have fewer rooms but book later.
  • Download offline maps for all of southern Utah before leaving Las Vegas. Cell coverage inside the parks is unreliable and in Canyonlands it is essentially absent.
  • Buy a cooler on day one. Breakfast and lunch out of the cooler is the single largest cost saving on this trip and it also keeps you out of a car for an extra hour each day.

Daily costs are per person, two travelers sharing a car and a room, and exclude airfare and the rental car. Full breakdown in the parks budget table.

The nine days

Day 1

Las Vegas to Springdale

Est. $142 / person
  • 10:00 AMCollect the rental car at LAS

    The consolidated rental center is a shuttle ride from the terminal. Budget 45 minutes more than you think.

    → 25 min drive
  • 11:15 AMGrocery, cooler and water run, $58

    A week of breakfasts, lunches and a case of water. Buy the cooler here, not at a park gateway town.

    → 2 hr 30 min drive
  • 02:30 PMCheck in, Springdale

    Park the car and leave it. The town shuttle connects to the park shuttle at the pedestrian entrance.

    → 15 min shuttle
  • 04:00 PMPa'rus Trail, 3.5 miles round trip

    Paved and flat. The best low-effort introduction to the canyon walls at golden hour.

    → 20 min shuttle back
  • 07:00 PMDinner in Springdale, $34

    Reserve ahead in season. Most kitchens stop serving by 9:00 PM.

Driving: 168 milesWalking: 3.5 milesPace: easy
Day 2

Zion Canyon, full day

Est. $118 / person
  • 06:30 AMFirst shuttle of the day

    Shuttles begin around 6:00 AM in peak season. Being on one of the first three is the entire strategy.

    → 40 min shuttle to stop 9
  • 07:30 AMRiverside Walk and Narrows entrance

    Two paved miles round trip. Wading further needs rented neoprene boots and a flash flood check.

    → 15 min shuttle
  • 10:30 AMEmerald Pools, lower and middle

    About three miles with modest climbing. Skip the upper pool if the group is flagging.

    → 20 min shuttle
  • 01:30 PMLunch on the lodge lawn, $22

    The only food inside the canyon. Otherwise it comes out of the cooler.

    → 25 min shuttle
  • 03:30 PMWatchman Trail, 3.3 miles

    Or Angels Landing if you drew a permit. That route has chains, real exposure and has caused fatalities.

Driving: 0 milesWalking: 8.4 milesPace: full
Day 3

Zion to Bryce Canyon

Est. $134 / person
  • 08:00 AMZion-Mount Carmel Highway east

    The 1930 tunnel has size restrictions and an escort fee for oversize vehicles. Standard SUVs pass freely.

    → 1 hr 45 min drive
  • 10:15 AMSunrise Point, Bryce Canyon

    The rim sits near 8,000 feet. It is roughly 15°F cooler than Zion and everyone is surprised by that.

    → 5 min walk
  • 11:00 AMQueen's Garden to Navajo Loop, 3 miles

    Down into the hoodoos and back up through Wall Street. The best short hike in the state.

    → 15 min drive
  • 02:30 PMScenic drive to Rainbow Point

    Eighteen miles each way with a dozen pullouts. Drive to the end, stop on the way back.

    → 20 min drive
  • 07:30 PMStargazing at Inspiration Point

    Bryce is a certified dark sky park. Bring every layer you packed. It gets genuinely cold after sunset.

Driving: 96 milesWalking: 4.8 milesPace: moderate
Day 4

Highway 12 to Capitol Reef

Est. $126 / person
  • 08:30 AMLeave Bryce on Highway 12

    One of the designated All-American Roads. The Hogback section has drop-offs on both sides and no shoulder.

    → 1 hr 15 min drive
  • 10:00 AMEscalante, Lower Calf Creek Falls

    Six miles round trip through a sandy canyon to a 126-foot waterfall. Three hours at a relaxed pace.

    → 1 hr 20 min drive
  • 02:30 PMBoulder Mountain summit pullouts

    The road climbs past 9,000 feet with aspen stands and a view across the Waterpocket Fold.

    → 45 min drive
  • 04:00 PMCheck in at Torrey, $28 dinner supplies

    Small town, a handful of good lodges and one genuinely excellent restaurant. Reserve dinner if you want it.

Driving: 118 milesWalking: 6.2 milesPace: moderate
Day 5

Capitol Reef, the park nobody stops at

Est. $98 / person
  • 08:00 AMScenic drive to Capitol Gorge

    Eight miles of pavement then a short graded dirt spur. Passenger cars are fine when dry.

    → 10 min walk
  • 09:30 AMCapitol Gorge Trail, 2 miles

    Flat wash walking past pioneer signatures carved into the canyon wall in the 1880s.

    → 25 min drive
  • 11:30 AMHickman Bridge, 1.8 miles

    A short climb to a 133-foot natural bridge. Almost empty compared to anything in Arches.

    → 10 min drive
  • 01:30 PMGifford House pie and the Fruita orchards, $14

    A working historic orchard inside the park. In season you can pick fruit and pay by the pound.

    → 2 hr 15 min drive
  • 05:30 PMArrive in Moab, check in, $32 dinner

    Three nights here. Unpack properly for the first time since Springdale.

Driving: 168 milesWalking: 4.4 milesPace: moderate
Day 6

Arches, early and late

Est. $104 / person
  • 06:45 AMEnter Arches before the timed-entry window

    Reservations typically apply from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM in peak months. Arriving before 7:00 AM avoids the requirement entirely.

    → 20 min drive
  • 07:30 AMDelicate Arch trail, 3 miles round trip

    Steep slickrock with no shade. Doing this at 7:30 AM instead of noon is the difference between a hike and an ordeal.

    → 25 min drive
  • 10:30 AMWindows Section loop, 1 mile

    Four large arches in a compact circuit. The best value per step in the park.

    → 30 min drive back to Moab
  • 12:30 PMMidday break in Moab, $24 lunch

    Deliberately off the trail during the hottest hours. This is how the trip stays enjoyable in May.

    → 35 min drive
  • 05:00 PMLandscape Arch and Devils Garden, 2 miles

    The longest arch in North America at 306 feet. Evening light and a fraction of the morning crowd.

Driving: 82 milesWalking: 6.1 milesPace: full
Day 7

Canyonlands, Island in the Sky

Est. $96 / person
  • 08:00 AMDrive up to the mesa

    The Island in the Sky district sits 2,000 feet above the rivers. The Needles district is a two-hour drive away and does not connect.

    → 45 min drive
  • 09:00 AMMesa Arch, 0.5 miles

    Famous at sunrise, which means fifty tripods. At 9:00 AM it is pleasant and nearly empty.

    → 20 min drive
  • 10:30 AMGrand View Point, 2 miles round trip

    A rim walk with a hundred-mile view over the confluence of the Green and Colorado rivers.

    → 25 min drive
  • 01:00 PMUpheaval Dome overlook, 1.5 miles

    Geologists still argue over whether it is a salt dome or a meteorite crater. The sign explains both.

    → 30 min drive
  • 04:30 PMDead Horse Point State Park, $20 per vehicle

    Not federal, so the parks pass does not cover it. Still the best sunset viewpoint in the region.

Driving: 124 milesWalking: 4.0 milesPace: moderate
Day 8

Rest day in Moab

Est. $88 / person
  • 09:30 AMSlow breakfast in town, $16

    Eight days in, this is the day that keeps the trip from turning into an endurance event.

    → 15 min drive
  • 11:00 AMColorado River float or a rest, $65 optional

    Half-day calm-water float trips run from town. Or do nothing at all, which is also a legitimate choice.

    → 20 min drive
  • 03:00 PMCorona Arch trail, 2.4 miles

    Outside the national park, free, and one of the best arches in the area. Includes a short ladder section.

    → 25 min drive
  • 07:00 PMDinner and repack, $38

    Tomorrow is a long driving day. Sort the cooler and settle the gas receipts tonight.

Driving: 46 milesWalking: 2.9 milesPace: easy
Day 9

Moab to Las Vegas

Est. $92 / person
  • 07:30 AMLeave Moab heading southwest

    The longest drive of the trip at roughly 460 miles. Start early and split it with two real stops.

    → 2 hr 30 min drive
  • 10:15 AMCoffee stop in Salina or Richfield, $12

    Last reliable services before a long empty stretch of I-70 and I-15.

    → 2 hr 15 min drive
  • 01:00 PMLunch in Cedar City, $18

    A pleasant college town and a better lunch stop than anything on the interstate.

    → 2 hr 45 min drive
  • 04:30 PMRefuel and return the rental car

    Fill up within ten miles of the airport and photograph the car from all four sides before handing over the keys.

    → 20 min shuttle
  • 06:30 PMEvening departure from LAS

    Give yourself three hours. The rental return shuttle plus security at LAS is not a place to be optimistic.

Driving: 462 milesWalking: 0.8 milesPace: driving day

How to adapt this

Only have seven days? Cut Capitol Reef and the rest day. Drive Bryce to Moab directly on Highway 12 and Highway 24 as one long scenic day. You lose the quietest park and the day that keeps everyone sane, so cut the rest day last.

Flying out of Salt Lake City instead? The loop becomes a line and day nine shortens to about four hours. Price the one-way rental drop fee first, because it often runs $150 to $300 and can wipe out the airfare saving.

Traveling with young kids? Replace Delicate Arch with the Windows loop and Sand Dune Arch, and add an afternoon at the Moab pool. Nobody under eight enjoys 3 miles of shadeless slickrock.

Going in summer? Move every hike before 10:00 AM, take a four-hour midday break, and add a gallon of water per person per day. Heat is the actual danger here, not anything dramatic.
