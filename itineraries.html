How to use a sample itinerary

Do not follow one exactly. Nobody's trip matches somebody else's energy level, budget or tolerance for early mornings. Use it as a spine and then cut.

The realistic method: open the itinerary, copy it into the planner, then delete the third thing on every day. Most people over-plan by about 30 percent, and the stops that survive that cut are the ones you actually wanted.

If you would rather have a person do this for you, our custom itinerary service builds a reviewed plan for a one-time fee starting at $149. See what that includes.

What every itinerary includes

  • Timed stops for each day
  • Travel time between consecutive stops
  • Estimated daily cost per person in USD
  • Driving or walking distance per day
  • A pace rating, from easy to full
  • Notes on what to book ahead and how far ahead
